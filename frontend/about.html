About Cinema Selector

The Project

Cinema Selector helps you decide what to watch. It recommends movies based on your watch history and preferences, so the more you use it, the better the picks get.

Rather than scrolling through a list, the app can randomly recommend a title from your library, keep a history of what you have already watched, and let you set viewing prefereneces (favorite genre, minimum rating, family-friendly filter) to tailor your future picks.
